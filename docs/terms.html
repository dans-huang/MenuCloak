What MenuCloak provides

MenuCloak is an open-source macOS utility that covers the application-menu area of the menu bar and displays user-provided focus text or optional information from a connected Google Calendar.

Google Calendar connection

The Google Calendar feature is optional and requires you to authorize read-only access through Google's OAuth consent flow. You may disconnect at any time. MenuCloak will not create, edit, or delete calendar events.
